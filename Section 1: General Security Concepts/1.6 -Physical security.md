# 🏢 Physical Security in IT

IT security isn't just digital – physical security matters too.

---

## **Barriers & Bollards**
- Bollards or barricades prevent vehicles from entering certain areas.
- Channel people through controlled access points.
- Can be concrete, water (with a bridge), or other methods.

---

## **Access Control Vestibules**
- Small rooms you must pass through to enter a building.
- Can be designed so only **one door opens at a time**.
- Often include **card readers or biometrics**.
- Common in **data centers** or high-security areas.

---

## **Fences**
- Common way to prevent access.
- Can be **transparent** (see-through) or **opaque** (cannot see through).
- High-security fences may have **razor wire** or be very tall.

---

## **Cameras & CCTV**
- Monitors specific areas.
- Can include **motion detection**, facial recognition, or license plate reading.
- Networked cameras can record video to a central location.
- Infrared cameras can detect in **low light or darkness**.

---

## **Security Guards**
- Monitor access points and enforce security.
- Two-person integrity ensures one guard cannot bypass rules.
- Guards may check IDs and grant temporary access.

---

## **Identification Badges**
- Worn visibly at all times.
- Integrated with electronic locks.
- Logs access to central databases.

---

## **Lighting**
- Well-lit areas deter unauthorized entry.
- Important for cameras and facial recognition.
- Infrared cameras can complement lighting for night monitoring.

---

## **Sensors & Motion Detection**
- **Infrared:** Detects motion in smaller areas.
- **Pressure sensors:** Detect movement across areas.
- **Microwave:** Covers large areas.
- **Ultrasonic:** Detects motion via sound waves, useful for collision detection.

---

## **Key Takeaway**
Physical security combines barriers, controlled access, cameras, lighting, and sensors to protect facilities and complement digital security.
